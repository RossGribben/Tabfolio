Privacy policy

Publisher: Ross Gribben · Effective date: 30 October 2026

Tabfolio Free & Tabfolio Plus

Your PDFs, notebooks and annotations stay in your local library on your PC. Tabfolio does not upload that content to the publisher.

Your documents and local library

Tabfolio is a Windows PDF organiser and notebook application. It reads files you choose to import and stores copies of imported PDFs, notebook text, annotations, stationery preferences and settings in your Windows user profile. These files may contain personal information that you choose to place in them.

The app does not require a Tabfolio account. It has no advertising, publisher-operated analytics or automatic cloud synchronisation, and the publisher does not sell your document data.

Exports, storage and deletion

PDF exports and .tabfolio backups are saved to the location you select. If you save them in a cloud-managed folder or send them to someone, that service or recipient handles those copies under their own policies.

Your local library remains on your device until deleted. Tabfolio does not provide its own document encryption; protection depends on your Windows account and device security. Removing an item from the library does not necessarily securely erase associated files or recovery copies. Original PDFs and exported backups remain separately until you delete them. Contact support for help removing local library data.

Microsoft Store purchases

Tabfolio uses Windows Store services to retrieve the price of Tabfolio Plus, request an optional one-time purchase, and check or restore ownership. Microsoft handles your Store account and payment information. Tabfolio does not receive your payment card details.

The app stores an encrypted local record of verified Plus access for offline use. This is separate from your documents and excluded from exported backups. Documents and notebook content are not sent with purchase requests. Microsoft handles Store purchases, downloads and Windows diagnostics under its own terms and privacy statement.

Support and privacy enquiries

Contact Ross Gribben at ruigolo@outlook.com.

If you email for help, the publisher receives your email address and whatever information you include, and uses it to respond, troubleshoot and handle follow-up questions. Please send only what is needed; do not include sensitive documents unless necessary for your request. Email providers process correspondence to deliver and store it.

Support correspondence is handled for the legitimate interests of answering enquiries and supporting the app. It is retained only as long as needed for the enquiry, related follow-up, or applicable legal obligations and disputes. You can contact the publisher to ask for access, correction or deletion of personal information held in support correspondence, or to object to or request restriction of its use, subject to applicable law. The publisher cannot remotely access or delete your local library.

If you have a privacy concern, please contact the publisher. You can also raise a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office.
